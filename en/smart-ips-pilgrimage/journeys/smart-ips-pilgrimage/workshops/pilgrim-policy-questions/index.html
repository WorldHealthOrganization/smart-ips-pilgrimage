    The pilgrim's journey: the policy questions

    The policy question each moment of the pilgrim's journey (P0 to P7) raises, one per moment, as questions for a room of origin- and host-country policy makers. A question is a question: the room names who holds the answer today, it does not decide it.

    For: Ministry of Health and pilgrimage-authority policy makers of an origin country and the host country
