Present a health credential

Skill holder-present-credential: The holder shows the one health credential a receiver asks for, as a QR: an HCERT (HC1:) on paper or in a wallet app, or a health link: the HCERT carrying a Verifiable Health Link, or a SMART Health Link (shlink:/). Input is what the holder holds and what is asked for; output is the QR shown. Performed by a person; the library has no holder-side operation.

Named by: Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent, Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent

Performed by a person: the holder (IHE ITI VHL VHL Holder, smart-trust Holder), often a parent presenting a child’s records. Role vhl-holder.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: the credentials held, each with the member it is about, its kind (hcert, vhl or shl), its medium (paper or wallet) and its qr text (HC1:… for an HCERT or VHL, shlink:/… for a SMART Health Link); and the request: which member, and which kind.
  • Out: the credential shown, and when it is a health link whose manifest needs a passcode (VHL, or an SHL with the P flag), that the holder must now say it (passcodeToSay).

Preconditions

The credential was issued to the holder (an HCERT by the issuing system, hcert-prefix-context; a VHL by the sharer, vhl-share; an SHL by its sharer, per the SMART Health Link specification).

What the holder does

  1. Pick the credential asked for, and only it: one member, one QR.
  2. Show the QR whole and steady (paper flat, screen bright); the receiver scans it (receiver-scan-credential).
  3. For a passcode-protected health link, say the passcode to the health worker; it is never printed beside the QR.

Success criteria

  • The receiver reads exactly the qr of the credential asked for.
  • Nothing about any other member is shown.
