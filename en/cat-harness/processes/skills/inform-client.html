Inform the client

Skill inform-client: A health worker tells the client (or caregiver) what was decided and what happens next, in words and, where the DAK gives one, on a slip or card: a referral, a return date, a result. Input is what to tell and the client’s language; output is that the client was told, and what they were handed. Generic for any DAK step that ends with the client being informed.

Named by: Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent, Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent, Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent, Health Check and SHL Issuance, Wallet Consent, Health Check and VHL Issuance, Wallet Consent

Skill id: inform-client · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Performed by a person in a health-worker lane. WHO’s immunization DAK has it as IMMZ.D7 “Counsel client” and IMMZ.D9 “Inform client when to return”.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: the about (a referral, a return visit, a result, a decision), the facts to convey, the language the client understands, and any handout the DAK gives (a referral slip, a vaccination card).
  • Out: informed (true once said and understood), the handedOver items, and the questions the client raised that need follow-up.

What the health worker does

  1. Say what was decided and what happens next, in the client’s language, without jargon; check it was understood.
  2. Hand over the slip or card when the DAK gives one.
  3. Note any question that needs a follow-up. Nothing else is recorded: this step adds no form.

Success criteria

  • The client can say where to go, when, and why.
  • Every handout the DAK names was given.
